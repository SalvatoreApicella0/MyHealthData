# Build 38 requirements

## Cuore e respiro
- Replace the heart-only dashboard art with a combined heart and lungs image.
- Keep respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, VO2 max, and six-minute walk in the merged module.

## Dolori corporei
- Selecting a history item highlights its exact marker on the 3D body.
- A selected item can be edited or deleted.
- The event editor uses progressive, compact controls with no redundant body-region field.

## Acqua e alcol
- The complete alcohol card is one hit target.
- Information is visually separate and never blocks the primary action.
- Reduce vertical height and keep water and alcohol close together.

## Vista
- Show right and left eye as the primary visual summary.
- Show current correction per eye immediately.
- Track prescriptions plus ocular conditions, symptoms, injuries, procedures, and notes.
- Support edit and delete for every record.

## Allergie, intestino, farmaci
- Replace squared text fields and long forms with rounded glass controls.
- Ask only essential information first; advanced details are disclosed progressively.
- Primary actions live in the content area, not as ambiguous toolbar text.

## Denti
- Replace the number-only grid with a recognizable upper/lower dental arch.
- Keep FDI numbers as secondary labels.
- Selecting a tooth opens its treatment history and entry flow.
- Add a prominent quick action for tooth brushing.

## Analisi del sangue
- OCR a classified blood-test attachment.
- Parse analyte, value, unit, reference range, and abnormal markers.
- Present a review screen before saving structured lab results.
- Never silently treat uncertain OCR output as verified clinical data.

## Navigation and dashboard editing
- Rename the quick tab to Home and the module directory to Salute.
- Use distinct native icons for Home, Salute, and Settings.
- Keep consistent module headers.
- Editing overlays must remain above both photo and glass cards.
